# Phase 09 — Latest News & Announcements

## Objective
Present real-time university notifications, examination alerts, and academic calendars in an editorial list format inspired by premier global institutions.

---

### 1. Section Header & Archive Action
**Includes**
- Eyebrow: "NOTICES & CIRCULARS"
- Primary Heading: "Latest University Announcements"
- Right-aligned Action Link: "View all notifications →"
- Section Canvas: Light-blue background (`--mist`, `#EAF2F8`)

---

### 2. Editorial Announcements Feed
**Includes**
- 4 Live-Style Notification Rows with Hairline Dividers:
  1. **Ph.D Entrance Examination 2026**
     - Date: *September 15, 2026* | Category: *Admissions*
     - Details: Comprehensive schedule, syllabus, and examination venue guidelines.
  2. **Postgraduate Common Entrance Test (PGCET) Calendar**
     - Date: *September 08, 2026* | Category: *Examinations*
     - Details: Revised counseling schedule and mandatory document verification dates.
  3. **Revaluation & Result Scrutiny Applications**
     - Date: *August 28, 2026* | Category: *Results*
     - Details: Extended deadline for submitting revaluation requests for even semester exams.
  4. **University Prospectus & Academic Calendar 2026–2027**
     - Date: *August 14, 2026* | Category: *General*
     - Details: Official release of the annual prospectus and institutional events calendar.

**Flow**
```
Visitor Scrolls to News Section
↓
Scans Chronological Announcement Rows
↓
Hovers Over Notification Row → Arrow Slides Right & Row Highlights
↓
Clicks Row / View All Notifications
```

---

## Phase 09 Output
At the end of this phase:
- Editorial list layout (not card blocks) renders cleanly on `--mist` background.
- Categories, dates, and titles are clearly structured for fast reading.
- Smooth hover animations on list rows.
