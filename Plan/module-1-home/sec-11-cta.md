# Phase 11 — Admissions Call to Action (CTA Banner)

## Objective
Drive prospective student conversion through a prominent, high-contrast banner with direct pathways to online application and prospectus download.

---

### 1. Banner Layout & Styling
**Includes**
- Background: Navy Deep (`--teal-deep`, `#071D33`) with subtle gold-accented radial background glow
- Padding: `84px 32px`
- Centered typography layout with responsive padding container

---

### 2. Messaging & Conversion Actions
**Includes**
- Eyebrow: "ADMISSIONS 2026–2027" in Gold (`--gold`)
- Serif Heading: "Begin your journey at Mangalagangothri" (`clamp(30px, 4vw, 46px)`)
- Subtext: "Applications for postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programmes are now open through the Karnataka UUCMS portal."
- Dual CTA Action Cluster:
  - **Primary CTA**: Gold Button ("Apply Now") with right-arrow icon and Navy text
  - **Secondary CTA**: Ghost Outlined Button ("Download Prospectus (PDF)") with download icon

**Flow**
```
Visitor Scrolls to Page Bottom
↓
Encounters Admissions Call to Action
↓
Clicks "Apply Now" (Launches Admissions Portal)
  OR
Clicks "Download Prospectus" (Fetches Academic Brochure)
```

---

## Phase 11 Output
At the end of this phase:
- High-contrast Navy Deep CTA banner renders with centered alignment.
- Primary Gold and Secondary Ghost buttons are styled and interactive with hover lifts.

---

## Out of Scope
- Dynamic PDF generation or backend UUCMS form processing.

---

## Phase 11 Completion
Phase 11 is complete when a visitor can:
```
View Admissions Banner
↓
Interact with "Apply Now" & "Download Prospectus" CTAs
```
Ready for Phase 12 (Institutional Footer).
