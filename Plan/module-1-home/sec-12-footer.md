# Phase 12 — Institutional Footer & Disclosures

## Objective
Provide an authoritative institutional anchor with complete statutory disclosures, academic faculty links, governance portals, and campus contact details.

  - NIRF Disclosures

**Flow**
```
Visitor Scrolls to Footer
↓

↓

```
