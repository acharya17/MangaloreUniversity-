# Phase 05 — Why Choose Mangalore University (Pillars)

## Objective
Articulate the three core institutional differentiators (Academic Depth, Research & Heritage, Campus Belonging) that position Mangalore University as a top destination for higher learning.

---

### 1. Section Header & Framing
**Includes**
- Eyebrow tag: "INSTITUTIONAL DISTINCTION" (`--blue`)
- Primary Heading: "Why Choose Mangalore University" (`.mu-heading`)
- Subtitle: "A confluence of traditional scholarly rigor, modern frontier research, and coastal student life."

  - Icon: `Compass` / `Trees` in circular badge
  - Title: Serene, Inspiring Living
