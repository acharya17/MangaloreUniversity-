# Phase 08 — Careers & Student Placement

## Objective
Present the university's career support infrastructure, competitive examination mentorship, and industry linkage bureau to build confidence in student graduate outcomes.

---

### 1. Left Column (Employment Bureau Overview)
**Includes**
- Eyebrow: "CAREER & STUDENT SUPPORT"
- Primary Heading: "University Employment Information & Guidance Bureau"
- Narrative: Dedicated to empowering students through campus recruitments, competitive civil services preparation, and global scholarship pathways.
- Action: "Connect with Placement Cell →"

---

### 2. Right Column (Structured Service Matrix)
**Includes**
- Vertical hairline-divided list rows (`border-bottom: 1px solid var(--line)`)
- Custom Gold Bullet Points (`--gold`) for each key service:
  1. **Campus Placement & Corporate Recruitment Drives** — Facilitating leading national and multinational campus hiring.
  2. **UPSC, KPSC, NET & SLET Coaching & Mentorship** — Dedicated coaching modules for national and state competitive exams.
  3. **Higher Education & Overseas Scholarship Counselling** — Expert advisory on international fellowships and research funding.
  4. **Skill Enhancement, Soft Skills & Internship Linkages** — Regular industry-led workshops and pre-placement training.

**Flow**
```
Visitor Views Career Bureau
↓
Reads Institutional Placement Narrative Left
↓
Reviews Structured 4-Point Service Matrix Right
```

---
